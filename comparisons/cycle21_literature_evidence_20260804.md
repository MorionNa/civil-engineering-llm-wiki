# Cycle 21 literature evidence

The new retrieval set contains three verified Nature Portfolio PDFs and one
cloned public repository. The evidence points to three complementary ideas:
local short-window/patch operators for scalable domain decomposition,
physics-driven recurrent convolutions for multiscale dynamics, and
power-flow messages for dynamics-aware graph propagation. A hyper-network
neural-ODE paper provides a useful analogy for replaceable local constitutive
representations.

These ideas do not by themselves prove the required matrix-edge hard-EOM
architecture. The current proposal must therefore keep MechConv force
assembly, the replaceable constitutive plugin, exactly two constitutive calls,
and direct inference. A power-flow feature may only influence a pre-final
constitutive kinematic/state path; a downstream force residual would violate
the intended physical interpretation.

The downloaded-paper manifest and cloned repository commit are recorded in
`docs/plans/cycle21_literature_evidence_20260804.md`.
