# Results

The paper evaluates Sep-PI-DeepONet on high-dimensional physics-informed operator learning problems.

Main findings:

- High-dimensional PDE learning becomes more scalable through separable representations.
- Physics constraints can be evaluated without directly suffering full dimensional growth.
- The method improves the feasibility of physics-informed neural operators for high-dimensional problems.

The paper provides official implementation for reproduction.
